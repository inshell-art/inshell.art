## What v1 was

The locked v1 artifact set is 16 topics in four groups. It explains Inshell
completely from the inside: the in-shell origin, anonymity as the inward
direction applied to the artist, Agent Art as an open field whose only
invariant is Agent participation, the THOUGHT–WILL–AWA arc, $PATH and Pulse,
and the records-and-verification systems beneath them.

Its gap is that it is almost entirely self-referential. A reader arriving
without context is told precisely what Inshell does and almost nothing about
the histories that make those choices legible as choices. Nothing explains
why SVG rather than any other format, why a token is used at all, what
generative art already settled, or what an Agent is as distinct from a model.

## What v2 adds

Seven topics, all in the `context` group, which is renamed from `Context` to
`Lineage and context`. Every existing topic is held unchanged in its claims.

| Slug | Title | What it places |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lineage` | Lineage | Instruction and execution: LeWitt's wall drawings, Fluxus event scores, Duchamp and Barthes on who completes a work |
| `generative-art` | Generative Art | 1965 and generative aesthetics; the Art Blocks seed pattern; why a seed is not an intention |
| `agents-and-ai` | Agents and AI | AARON, the GAN authorship dispute, diffusion, and why Inshell says Agent rather than AI |
| `svg` | SVG | The W3C format history and the four kinds of reader an SVG has |
| `ethereum` | Ethereum | The EVM as a deterministic public machine and gas as a formal bound |
| `tokens-and-nfts` | Tokens and NFTs | ERC-721, the metadata pointer, and pointer decay |
| `onchain-art` | Onchain Art | Degrees of onchain, public precedents, and where Inshell sits |

The arc runs art → machines → material → substrate → record → field, then
closes on the existing `design-principles` as synthesis.

## Editorial discipline held

Every new topic carries `artist-editorial` authority only, and every one ends
with an explicit evidence boundary stating that the works, dates, and figures
named are public references to other people's practices which Inshell has not
verified, does not endorse, and claims no affiliation with.

This follows the rule already recorded in `docs/AGENTS.md`: distinguish an
Inshell method from a field definition. `svg` states outright that SVG is not
required for fully onchain work and not required for Agent Art. `onchain-art`
repeats that fully onchain is a chain-sufficiency claim rather than a synonym
for immutable, decentralized, deployed, or verified.

The sharpest new claim is in `generative-art`: seeded generative work can be
regenerated from its seed, so storing the rule and the seed suffices, whereas a
human–Agent exchange cannot be regenerated because the exchange is the content.
That is stated as a difference between two methods, not a ranking.

No new authority class and no new group id were introduced, because both enums
are fixed in the generator, which is outside this change's boundary.
